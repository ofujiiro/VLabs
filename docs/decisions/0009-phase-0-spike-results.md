# 0009. Results of the three phase 0 risk spikes

- **Status:** in progress, November 2026

The brief asks for three throwaway spikes before phase 1: (a) low-latency
audio output on Windows and Linux; (b) hosting a CLAP and a VST3 instrument;
(c) a piano roll with 10,000 notes that scrolls and zooms smoothly. This
record collects what was measured, what passed, and what could not be
verified. It is completed when all three spikes have run on both systems.
