# VLabs 1.0

The first release of VLabs, an easy, open-source music workstation for
Windows and Linux. Build a beat on a step grid, write melodies and chords
in a piano roll, record your voice or an instrument, arrange it all on one
timeline, mix it and export it, and have a voicebank sing your melodies.
It works on a plain computer: speakers or headphones, a mouse and a
keyboard. A MIDI keyboard or an audio interface is used when you plug one
in, but you never need one.

## Download

- **Windows 10 or 11:** `VLabs-1.0.0-setup.exe`. It installs VLabs for
  you alone, needs no administrator rights, and makes VLabs songs open
  with a double-click. Windows may warn that the installer is from an
  unknown publisher (it is not signed yet): choose **More info**, then
  **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** `VLabs-1.0.0-x86_64.AppImage`. Make it executable (in your
  file manager's properties, or `chmod +x` in a terminal) and double-click
  it. It runs on most distributions from 2024 on (glibc 2.39 or newer).
- The same program without an installer: `VLabs-1.0.0-windows.zip` and
  `VLabs-1.0.0-linux.tar.gz`.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists each file's checksum.

Press **F1** in the program for the user guide.

## What it does

- **Beats and melodies:** a step grid with a recorded acoustic kit and an
  electronic kit, or a drum kit of your own sounds; a piano roll with the
  included piano, a synthesizer of its own (two oscillators, a filter,
  envelopes, an LFO and presets), SoundFont and SFZ instruments, or CLAP
  and VST3 plugins.
- **Music theory made easy:** give the song a key and the piano roll shows
  its notes, keeps new notes in it and places whole chords with one click;
  transpose by intervals; triplets, quintuplets and septuplets; swing.
- **Tempo:** change the tempo and the time signature anywhere, suddenly or
  gradually, with the shape of the change you want.
- **Recording:** notes from the computer keyboard or a MIDI keyboard, with
  a metronome and count-in; sound from the microphone or an audio
  interface.
- **Audio clips:** bring in WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3 and Ogg files; cut, trim
  and fade clips right on the timeline.
- **Arranging and mixing:** one timeline for patterns and audio; volume,
  pan, mute, solo and an effect chain per track (EQ, compressor, reverb,
  delay, limiter and plugin effects); automation for any setting.
- **Plugins and sounds:** drop a downloaded plugin on the window to add it;
  a Plugins window to manage them (Windows plugins on Linux through Wine
  and yabridge); a sound library to browse and hear folders of samples and
  drag them into the song.
- **Singing:** add classic UTAU voicebanks, give notes lyrics (romaji or
  kana) and a voice track sings them. Shape how: bend the sung pitch where
  you click, add vibrato, make notes louder, lighter, darker or breathier.
  The voicebank's picture shows faintly on its track.
- **Files:** songs are saved with their sound inside; your work is saved
  automatically, even a recording in progress, and offered back after a
  crash. Export WAV, FLAC, MIDI (with lyrics) and DAWproject files (opened
  by Bitwig Studio, Studio One and Cubase); open MIDI and DAWproject files
  from other programs.
- **Everything can be undone.**

## Good to know

- VST2 plugins are not supported; CLAP and VST3 are. A plugin must be made
  for your system (Windows or Linux), or go through Wine and yabridge on
  Linux.
- Singing understands Japanese lyrics (romaji and kana) and classic UTAU
  voicebanks; UST and USTX projects and DiffSinger voices come later.
  Read each voicebank's terms: they say what you may do with the voice.
- The installer is not signed, hence Windows' warning.
- Songs saved by VLabs 1.0 will open in later versions.
