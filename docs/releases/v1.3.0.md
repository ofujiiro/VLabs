# VLabs 1.3

Play VLabs from a MIDI controller: the pitch and modulation wheels,
pressure and pedals play the instrument in focus and are recorded with your
notes, its transport buttons play and record, and any knob, fader, button
or pad can be given a job by moving it.

## Download

- **Windows 10 or 11:** `VLabs-1.3.0-setup.exe`. It installs VLabs for
  you alone, needs no administrator rights, and makes VLabs songs open
  with a double-click. Windows may warn that the installer is from an
  unknown publisher (it is not signed yet): choose **More info**, then
  **Run anyway**. Installing it over an earlier version keeps your
  settings and songs.
- **Linux:** `VLabs-1.3.0-x86_64.AppImage`. Make it executable (in your
  file manager's properties, or `chmod +x` in a terminal) and double-click
  it. It runs on most distributions from 2024 on (glibc 2.39 or newer).
- The same program without an installer: `VLabs-1.3.0-windows.zip` and
  `VLabs-1.3.0-linux.tar.gz`.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists each file's checksum.

Press **F1** in the program for the user guide.

## What is new

- **The wheels and pedals play.** The pitch wheel bends the notes (two
  semitones each way on the piano, SoundFonts and the synth; an SFZ
  instrument as far as it says; drums are not bent). The modulation wheel,
  and pressure on keyboards with aftertouch, add vibrato; an expression
  pedal shapes the loudness. Plugins receive everything your controller
  sends, so their own MIDI learn gives their knobs to yours.
- **Takes keep what you played.** Recording notes also records the wheels,
  pressure and pedals. Under the piano roll's notes, **Bend** and **Mod**
  show them as curves: add points with a click, drag them, remove them with
  a right-click, or draw bends without a controller.
- **Transport buttons** that send MIDI Machine Control, or Start and Stop,
  play, stop, record and go back to the start.
- **Give a knob or fader a job:** right-click a mixer fader or pan, or a
  setting in an effect's or the synth's window, choose **Assign a MIDI
  control…**, and move a knob. **Give a button or pad a command** (play,
  record, loop, undo…) with **Learn** in Keyboard shortcuts.
- **MIDI controllers…** in the menu lists your controllers (switch one off
  to leave it to another program) and what their controls do.
- **Fixed:** a key held while you clicked another track could sound for
  ever.
- **MIDI files** now carry pitch bends and controllers both ways, and a
  file's sustain pedal holds its notes when it comes in.

## Good to know

- Songs open in 1.3 whichever version saved them. A song with recorded or
  drawn curves, or with knobs given settings, is saved in a newer format
  that 1.2 and earlier do not open.
- Endless knobs must be set to "absolute" on the controller to move a
  setting.
- The installer is not signed, hence Windows' warning.
