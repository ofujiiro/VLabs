# VLabs 1.2

Move several things at once: select several tracks and drag them to
another place in the list, or several clips and move, copy or remove them
together. Exporting now plays the song once from its start and ends with
its last sound, even when a loop is set.

## Download

- **Windows 10 or 11:** `VLabs-1.2.0-setup.exe`. It installs VLabs for
  you alone, needs no administrator rights, and makes VLabs songs open
  with a double-click. Windows may warn that the installer is from an
  unknown publisher (it is not signed yet): choose **More info**, then
  **Run anyway**. Installing it over an earlier version keeps your
  settings and songs.
- **Linux:** `VLabs-1.2.0-x86_64.AppImage`. Make it executable (in your
  file manager's properties, or `chmod +x` in a terminal) and double-click
  it. It runs on most distributions from 2024 on (glibc 2.39 or newer).
- The same program without an installer: `VLabs-1.2.0-windows.zip` and
  `VLabs-1.2.0-linux.tar.gz`.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists each file's checksum.

Press **F1** in the program for the user guide.

## What is new

- **Several tracks at once.** Click a track's name to select it,
  **Ctrl**+click others to add or remove them, **Shift**+click to select
  every track between. Drag a selected track's name up or down: the
  selected tracks move there together, in their order. The mixer follows
  the new order.
- **Several clips at once.** **Ctrl**+click clips to select them, or hold
  **Ctrl** (or **Shift**) and drag from an empty spot to draw a box around
  them; **Ctrl+A** selects them all. Drag one of them by its middle and
  they all move together; **Delete** removes them, **Ctrl+D** places a
  copy right after them, and the keys that move a clip by a bar move them
  all. **Escape** clears the selection.
- **Fixed: the export looped.** With a loop set, an exported song went
  back to the loop's start and kept playing it for several seconds. The
  export now plays the song once from its start to its end, and the file
  ends where the last sound does, with no silence after it.

## Good to know

- Songs open in 1.2 whichever version saved them; 1.2 saves them as 1.1
  does.
- Several selected clips move in time only; a single sound clip can still
  be dragged to another audio track.
- The installer is not signed, hence Windows' warning.
