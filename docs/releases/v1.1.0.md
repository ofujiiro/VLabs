# VLabs 1.1

Shape your sounds: decide how an instrument's notes start and end (a
"release cut" piano in one click), and open any sound clip in a sound
editor to cut it exactly, fade it, play it backwards, change its pitch or
make it as loud as it can be. This version also fixes a crash when adding
a second folder to the sound library.

## Download

- **Windows 10 or 11:** `VLabs-1.1.0-setup.exe`. It installs VLabs for
  you alone, needs no administrator rights, and makes VLabs songs open
  with a double-click. Windows may warn that the installer is from an
  unknown publisher (it is not signed yet): choose **More info**, then
  **Run anyway**. Installing it over 1.0 keeps your settings and songs.
- **Linux:** `VLabs-1.1.0-x86_64.AppImage`. Make it executable (in your
  file manager's properties, or `chmod +x` in a terminal) and double-click
  it. It runs on most distributions from 2024 on (glibc 2.39 or newer).
- The same program without an installer: `VLabs-1.1.0-windows.zip` and
  `VLabs-1.1.0-linux.tar.gz`.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists each file's checksum.

Press **F1** in the program for the user guide.

## What is new

- **Note shape.** The piano, SoundFonts, SFZ instruments, drum kits and
  custom kits have a **Note shape** button (a small rise-and-fall line) in
  their track's header and next to the track's name above its pattern.
  Set how long notes take to rise (**Attack**) and to fall silent once
  their key comes up (**Release**), or pick one of four quick choices:
  - **Cut at note end**: every note stops the moment its key comes up,
    with no ringing tail (the "release cut" sound);
  - **Soft start**: notes swell in;
  - **Long tail**: notes ring on;
  - **Natural**: as the instrument plays them.

  On a kit, a release also makes each hit stop at the end of its note, so
  shortening a note cuts its sound. With a release set, a SoundFont's own
  reverb is left out so a cut sounds cut; add a Reverb effect to the track
  for a room.
- **Sound editor.** Double-click an audio clip (or choose **Edit sound…**
  in its right-click menu): a window shows its whole sound, with the part
  the clip plays lit. Drag its ends to cut it exactly (they do not snap to
  beats there), drag the small squares to fade it, and change its
  **Volume**, its **Pitch** in semitones (like a tape, the sound also plays
  faster or slower), play it **Backwards**, or make it **As loud as it can
  be**. Each change can be undone.
- **Fixed:** adding a second folder to the sound library no longer closes
  the program.

## Good to know

- Songs saved by 1.0 open in 1.1. Songs saved by 1.1 need 1.1 or later:
  1.0 says they were saved by a newer version and does not open them.
- Changing a clip's pitch changes its length too (as a tape does);
  changing pitch while keeping the length comes later.
- DAWproject export keeps neither note shapes nor backwards or re-pitched
  clips (they go out forwards at their own pitch).
- The installer is not signed, hence Windows' warning.
