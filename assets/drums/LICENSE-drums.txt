acoustic.kit — the bundled acoustic drum kit

Built from Big Rusty Drums by Karoryfer Samples,
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums (commit
f07ce00df34a46b6b08375be56fe116cf15782bc).

Big Rusty Drums is dedicated to the public domain under the Creative
Commons CC0 1.0 Universal public domain dedication:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The processed kit (acoustic.kit) is likewise dedicated to the public domain
under CC0 1.0. How it was made is described in THIRD_PARTY.md and in
tools/make-drums.

The electronic kit and the clap and cowbell of the acoustic kit are not
recordings: they are synthesised by the program's own code (crates/daw-dsp,
src/drums/synth.rs) and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 too.
